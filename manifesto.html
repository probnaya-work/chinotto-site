the manifesto

Chinotto is for thoughts that stop mid-sentence — and for returning to them when more has gathered around them.

Most notes apps help you capture, organize, or find a note later.

Returning to an unfinished thought — with more around it — is rarer:

How do you pick up where you left off?

Most notes apps keep the file. Chinotto keeps the return — the same line, later, with more weight.

Each moment keeps its date. Continue adds a new moment, dated now. Correct changes the wording while keeping the original date and earlier words.

On Mac, read back through the Record, follow a Line, or find words you remember. A Return brings an older moment back when recent writing gives it a reason.

On iPhone, Chinotto is the pocket — capture with widget, voice, or share-in; Find and Continue are there when you want to pick something up.

Not another notes app. A place to pick up unfinished thoughts months later.

Capture needs no account. Optional sync uses Sign in with Apple to carry words, dates, Lines and wording history between iPhone and Mac through the cloud. Original recordings stay on the device that captured them.

Local capture, reading, Find and audio playback need no subscription. Chinotto Pro enables iPhone sync. Sync on Mac is free; available iPhone plans and prices are shown before purchase.

A private record of thoughts, encounters, and what continues between them.
