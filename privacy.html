Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 September 2026.

This Privacy Policy covers Chinotto for Mac and Chinotto for iOS. Both are local-first applications: by default, your thoughts are stored on your device and are not collected by us.

Local-first by default

All entries are stored locally on your device. Core capture, search, and browsing your history work fully offline. You can use either app without creating an account or signing in.

Voice and transcription

In Chinotto for iOS 2.0.1 and later, transcription runs only on your iPhone. Chinotto uses Apple's speech recognition with on-device recognition required. If your iPhone can't recognise the language you speak on the device, or speech recognition isn't allowed, the recording stays on your iPhone without words, and Chinotto tries again on the iPhone later if on-device recognition becomes available. Your recordings are never sent to Apple or to any other transcription server.

Chinotto for iOS versions earlier than 2.0.1, and the direct-download Chinotto for Mac released so far (up to version 3.0.0), recognise speech on your device when your device supports the language you speak, and may otherwise use Apple's speech recognition service, which then receives that audio.

The recording itself is kept in Chinotto's app data on your device. It is not part of sync — only the transcribed text syncs, the same as any other entry. Backups your device makes, such as iCloud Backup or Time Machine, may include this app data.

In Chinotto for iOS 2.0.1 and later, when you remove a voice entry you have 8 seconds to bring it back; after that, its recording and its transcript are deleted from your iPhone. In the earlier versions named above, removing an entry may leave its recording in Chinotto's app data on that device until the app's data is removed. For the synced copy of an entry, see Deleting your data below.

Data collection

Chinotto may collect minimal anonymous usage data to understand how the product is used and improve the experience.

We do not collect:

  • the content of your thoughts for analytics
  • personal information
  • search queries
  • identifiable data

Signing in and sync

Sync is optional on both apps and is enabled only when you sign in with Apple. When you enable sync, your entries are transmitted and stored so they are available across your devices, along with a device name and identifier so you can see and manage which devices are connected.

Sync is free on Mac. On iOS, Chinotto Pro is required to enable sync; nothing else on iOS is gated — capture, voice, search, browsing history, and the Home Screen widget all work without a subscription or an account.

Deleting your data

You can delete your cloud account and synced data from within either app. This removes the copy of your record held in the cloud; the record on your own devices is untouched.

When sync is on, removing an entry marks it as removed in the synced copy; its text stays there until you delete your cloud account.

Sharing a thread

If you create a share link from the desktop app, a copy of that thread is stored so the link can be opened by anyone who has it. Shared pages are excluded from search engines. The copy is deleted when you revoke the link or when it expires.

Third-party services

We use Umami for anonymous, opt-in product analytics; it does not receive your entry content and is not used for advertising or tracking. We do not use Firebase Analytics. Sign-in and sync run on Firebase; on iOS, Apple and our subscription provider process your purchase and subscription status. Voice transcription uses Apple Speech, part of iOS and macOS. In Chinotto for iOS 2.0.1 and later it runs only on your iPhone, and no audio reaches Apple. In the earlier versions named under Voice and transcription, when it uses Apple's speech recognition service, Apple processes that audio under Apple's own privacy policy. Apart from that, none of these services receive your thought content beyond what sync itself requires.
