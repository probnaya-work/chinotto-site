privacy

Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy covers Chinotto for Mac and Chinotto for iOS. Both are local-first applications: by default, your thoughts are stored on your device and are not collected by us.

Local-first by default

All entries are stored locally on your device. Core capture, search, and browsing your history work fully offline. You can use either app without creating an account or signing in.

Voice and transcription

Voice recordings you capture, on Mac or iOS, stay on your device. Transcription happens on-device. The recording itself is never sent anywhere, even when sync is on — only the transcribed text syncs, the same as any other entry.

Data collection

Chinotto may collect minimal anonymous usage data to understand how the product is used and improve the experience.

We do not collect:

  • the content of your thoughts for analytics
  • personal information
  • search queries
  • identifiable data

Signing in and sync

Sync is optional on both apps and is enabled only when you sign in with Apple. When you enable sync, your entries are transmitted and stored so they are available across your devices, along with a device name and identifier so you can see and manage which devices are connected.

Sync is free on Mac. On iOS, Chinotto Pro is required to enable sync; nothing else on iOS is gated — capture, voice, search, browsing history, and the Home Screen widget all work without a subscription or an account.

Deleting your data

You can delete your cloud account and synced data from within either app. This removes the copy of your record held in the cloud; the record on your own devices is untouched.

Sharing a thread

If you create a share link from the desktop app, a copy of that thread is stored so the link can be opened by anyone who has it. Shared pages are excluded from search engines. The copy is deleted when you revoke the link or when it expires.

Third-party services

We use Umami for anonymous, opt-in product analytics; it does not receive your entry content and is not used for advertising or tracking. We do not use Firebase Analytics. Sign-in and sync run on Firebase; on iOS, Apple and our subscription provider process your purchase and subscription status. None of these services receive your thought content beyond what sync itself requires.

Contact

For questions about this Privacy Policy, contact: mail@probnaya.work — see also support.
